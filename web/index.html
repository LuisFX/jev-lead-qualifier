    Privacy

    What leaves the building

    • Sent to Jev: company description, person name, title and bio, the message text, and the ICP option texts. Never e-mail addresses or phone numbers.
    • Nothing is generated: Jev returns probabilities over options you defined. Decline drafts are templates filled in code and are never auto-sent.
    • Answers are stored per lead and question in the sqlite ledger; they survive a restart.
    • A message that asks for credentials or personal data is flagged for security review and never routed automatically.
